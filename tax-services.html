Tax Preparation & Support

Program M provides tax preparation and support services designed around accuracy, clear communication, and organized recordkeeping — for individuals and businesses alike.

Individual Tax Preparation

Personal income tax return preparation with clear explanations of what's being filed and why.

Self-Employed Tax Preparation

Support for freelancers, gig workers, and independent contractors navigating self-employment tax obligations.

Small-Business Tax Preparation

Tax preparation support scaled to small-business structures and recordkeeping needs.

Tax Consultation

General consultation to help clients understand their filing obligations and options.

Cryptocurrency Tax Preparation

Support for reporting cryptocurrency transactions as part of an individual or business return.

Tax Document Organization

Help gathering, organizing, and preparing documentation ahead of filing.

IRS Correspondence Support

Assistance reviewing and responding to IRS notices. As an AFSP Record of Completion holder, representation is limited to returns personally prepared and signed.

Tax Planning & Education

General, forward-looking guidance to help clients plan ahead, where legally permissible.
