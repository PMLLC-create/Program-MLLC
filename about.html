About Program Management LLC

Program Management LLC, a Texas limited liability company founded in March 2019, was established to provide dependable professional, tax, and financial support while helping individuals, businesses, and organizations better understand and manage their responsibilities. The company provides tax and financial services to individual and small-business clients under its operating name, Program M Tax & Financial. The company is faith-based and operates on values that shape every client relationship.

Program M Tax & Financial is an operating name of Program Management LLC and is not a separate legal entity.

Quintina S. Simpson Njoku

Founder · Program Management LLC, est. March 2019

Quintina S. Simpson Njoku is the founder of Program Management LLC and Program M Tax & Financial, established in 2019. She is a PTIN holder and has completed the IRS Annual Filing Season Program (AFSP). With a background in psychology, ministry, and business, Quintina takes a people-centered approach to tax and financial services. Her goal is to provide clients with knowledgeable, transparent, and trustworthy service while helping individuals, families, and business owners better understand their financial responsibilities. As a faith-based business owner, she operates with a commitment to integrity, confidentiality, education, and genuine care for every client.
