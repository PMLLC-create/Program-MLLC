Tax-Season Checklist

A general starting point for gathering what your preparer will need — organized so you can work through it category by category before your appointment.

Personal information

  • Government-issued photo ID for yourself (and spouse, if filing jointly)
  • Social Security cards or ITIN letters for yourself, spouse, and dependents
  • Last year's tax return, for reference
  • Direct deposit information for any refund

Income documents

  • W-2s from every employer
  • 1099-NEC or 1099-MISC for contract/freelance work
  • 1099-K for payment-app or marketplace income, if applicable
  • 1099-INT / 1099-DIV for interest and dividends
  • 1099-R for retirement or pension distributions
  • SSA-1099 for Social Security benefits
  • Records of any cryptocurrency transactions
  • Rental income records, if applicable
  • Unemployment compensation statements (1099-G), if applicable

Self-employed & small-business clients

  • Total income received for the year
  • Business expense records, organized by category
  • Mileage log or total business miles driven
  • Home office measurements, if claiming that deduction
  • Records of any estimated tax payments made
  • Business bank and credit card statements
  • Asset purchases made during the year (equipment, software, vehicles)

Deductions & credits

  • Mortgage interest statement (Form 1098) and property tax records
  • Student loan interest statement (Form 1098-E)
  • Tuition statements (Form 1098-T) and education expenses
  • Childcare provider information and amount paid
  • Charitable donation receipts
  • Significant medical expense records
  • Retirement contribution records
  • Health insurance marketplace statement (Form 1095-A), if applicable

Before your appointment

Organize documents by the categories above, note any life changes from the past year — marriage, a new child, a new business, a home purchase, a change of address — and write down any questions you want to cover. Bringing things organized, even loosely, saves time and helps your return get done accurately the first time.

This checklist is general educational information, not individualized tax advice, and does not cover every situation. Requirements vary by circumstance — confirm what applies to you with your preparer.
